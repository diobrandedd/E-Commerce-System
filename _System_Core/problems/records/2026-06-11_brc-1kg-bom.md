# Problem: BRC supplier price assumed 20 kg bag

> Real supplier unit: **1 kg bag** (same as PT / Jar Cacao), not 20 kg.

## Symptom

Supplier Master ₱/bag and stock-in yield treated one bag as 20 kg → 200 pouches. Costing formula used **0.005** bag/pouch, understating raw cost per pouch by 20×.

## Cause

Phase 18 BRC rollout used placeholder 20 kg bag math before ops confirmed 1 kg supplier unit.

## Fix

- `BRC_GRAMS_PER_BAG = 1000` in `dept_products.php` + `deptProducts.ts`
- `migration_phase23_brc_1kg_bom.sql` — formula raw qty **0.1**; recalc `baseline_cost`
- Updated [[specs/2026-06-05_brc-bom-math]]
