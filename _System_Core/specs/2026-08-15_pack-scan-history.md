# Pack Scan History Report

## Summary

Read-only History tab on Deliveries → Pack scan. Lists confirmed honey bottle pack sessions with who confirmed, when, bottle counts, and per-batch / per-bottle detail. Filters: date range and batch code search. Does not modify existing live pack-scan rows.

## Roles

Same as Pack scan: executive, prod_manager, staff.

## Out of scope

- Mutating or deleting historical sessions
- Dept pouch/jar pack history (honey bottles only)
- New sidebar nav item
